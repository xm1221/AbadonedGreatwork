      Items

      I devote this section to the magical and mysterious items I might encounter in my studies.

      Truth Crystal

      One of the relics left by ancient hexcasters is the Truth Crystal. These mysterious crystals appear to have been used as testing tools — designed to evaluate the skill of an apprentice by challenging them to produce specific spell patterns.

      A Truth Crystal contains a riddle with a description, and a length limit for submitted patterns. The aspiring hexcaster must solve the riddle to discover the correct pattern list.


      To attempt a riddle, place the Truth Crystal into a Truth Crystal Base, then right-click the base while holding an Iota-containing item. The crystal will evaluate your spell and judge the result.

      If successful, the crystal will be consumed and a reward will be granted from a loot table specific to that crystal.

      Hex Eyes

      Eye of Tracking

      An Eye of Tracking hovers near a chosen creature and follows it wherever it goes, like a patient vulture. It never shatters on its own — it simply keeps watch until its target is gone.


      Eye of Waypoint

      An Eye of Waypoint flies to a chosen place and hovers there, marking the spot. Once it arrives it lingers a while, then breaks with a burst of light.

              Abandoned Greatwork

              The ancients left behind, and those who come after follow. I am neither the first Hex Caster, nor will I be the last.

              Across the world, ruins left by ancient Hex Casters are scattered about. Though abandoned and cast into the mundane world, these structures still reflect the glory and brilliance they once held as Greatworks.


              It seems the Hex Casters of old also created a special material to test the skill of their apprentices. Those who passed the trial would receive a reward.

              Unfortunately, my records on this are not yet detailed enough. I should conduct more in-depth research when the opportunity arises.

                          Locate: takes a Biome Token (a ResourceLocation wrapping a biome or structure), seeks its nearest occurrence across the wide world, and returns its position vector. Misfortune strikes if none can be found.


                          Eye of Locating

                          An Eye of Locating, thrown like an Eye of Ender, flies a short distance, then hovers and searches the surroundings. If it senses the target biome or structure nearby, it turns to face it. After a while it shatters — or, more often, drops back as an item still bearing its target, ready to be thrown again.
