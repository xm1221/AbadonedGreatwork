      Truth Crystal

      One of the relics left by ancient hexcasters is the Truth Crystal. These mysterious crystals appear to have been used as testing tools — designed to evaluate the skill of an apprentice by challenging them to produce specific spell patterns.

      A Truth Crystal contains a riddle with a description, and a length limit for submitted patterns. The aspiring hexcaster must solve the riddle to discover the correct pattern list.


      To attempt a riddle, place the Truth Crystal into a Truth Crystal Base, then right-click the base while holding an Iota-containing item. The crystal will evaluate your spell and judge the result.

      If successful, the crystal will be consumed and a reward will be granted from a loot table specific to that crystal.

      Confluence Directrix

      An ordinary Directrix chooses only one path at a time. Yet after studying fragments from ancient ruins, I realized a media wave can branch along multiple routes simultaneously — provided a mind both sharp enough and divided enough guides it.

      An ordinary villager's mind falters when split too far; only an illager practiced in commanding multiple manifestations at once can endure such division.

      Empty Confluence Directrix

      Combining an Empty Directrix with emeralds and charged amethyst yields an Empty Confluence Directrix.

      Sneak and Use Item/Place Block any of its six faces to cycle that face through Unconnected, Input, and Output.


      When a media wave enters an Empty Confluence Directrix, it forks copies of the stack and Ravenmind across every connected Output face in true parallel; the Impetus finishes only after every active branch concludes.

      With two or more Input faces, earlier waves wait at the node until all inputs arrive: their stacks concatenate bottom-to-top in arrival order, while the final arrival's Ravenmind prevails. However, should media waves from different Impetuses collide at the same Confluence Directrix, the conflicting sources immediately trigger a Mishap!

      Casting Flay Mind on an Evoker into a placed Empty Confluence Directrix awakens its inner core into an Evoker's Confluence Directrix, preserving existing port settings.

      On Merge (2+ inputs): the raw stack is replaced by one list per arrival in arrival order, each formatted as [stack_list, ravenmind_or_null, enter_dir_vec], and the stored pattern executes once.

      On Split: for each valid Output face, its outgoing unit direction vector is pushed onto an isolated copy before executing the stored pattern once per branch; a mishap stops only that branch.

      Media Wave Repeater

      Forging a Goat Horn with Slates, Charged Amethyst, and Quenched Allay Shards yields a Media Wave Repeater for long-range links.


      Can sit upright or upside-down across four horizontal facings.
      Standing alone, it catches matching waves and relays them 2 ticks later with 1.25× forward range ahead of the horn (+4 equivalent slates, min 5, up to 8 loop-free hops).
      Wired into slates directly, it also serves as a forward-boosted directional transmitter.

      Flaying the Mind of any Illager (Pillager, Vindicator, Evoker, or Illusioner) into an Empty Impetus produces a Pillager Impetus.

      Media Pool

      Even the grandest spell circles often choke on an Impetus's limited media reserve before their work concludes.

      By shaping quenched crystal and slate around a Phial of Media, I built an open reservoir capable of exchanging media cleanly with waves passing through a circle.

      Media Pool

      Ring a Phial of Media with Quenched Allay Shards and Slates to craft a Media Pool.


      Must be placed flat upright; holds up to 7680 Amethyst Dust (or unlimited if fueled with a Creative Unlocker). Right-click with Amethyst Dust, Shards, Charged Amethyst, or media holders to refill. Its liquid surface rises and falls with reserve, comparators read 0–15, and a Scrying Lens shows exact stored Dust.


      When a spell wave passes through a Media Pool, it pops a finite number n from the stack top:

      n > 0: moves n Dust from the pool into the circle's Impetus (Mishap if pool lacks media);

      n < 0: draws |n| Dust from the Impetus into the pool (Mishap if Impetus lacks media);

      n = 0: pops zero and passes through; an empty stack or non-number triggers a Mishap.


      Vindicator's Ambit Directrix

      Evokers split their focus across minions, and Pillagers hail distant raids; yet Vindicators are the most obsessive wardens of boundaries among illagers—guarding a single woodland corridor with axe raised to cleave life from death.

      Flaying that territorial obsession into a directrix lets us carve a circle's rigid rectangular bounds into custom contours without conjuring a single block of extra volume.

      Waymark Pylon
      4

      Combine Amethyst Shards, Slate, Iron Ingots, and Deepslate to forge four slender Waymark Pylons.


      Empty-hand Use Item/Place Block cycles channels 0..15, or write any custom Iota for private tuning. Using Waymark Pylons on a placed Vindicator's Ambit Directrix imprints its channel onto the item stack; placed pylons auto-append #1, #2, #3... in order, while sneaking empty-hand Use Item/Place Block cycles a pylon's chain rank.


      When a media wave traverses the directrix, it links all matching pylons into an even-odd polygon (supporting arbitrary concave C-shapes and crossing strokes without error) extruded from the lowest to highest pylon, replacing the branch's default box.
      An empty channel (0 pylons) restores default bounds; 1–2 pylons or a volume S × H exceeding the original circle box triggers a Mishap.

      Eye of Tracking

      An Eye of Tracking chases the target creature relentlessly. Once it reaches a spot above it, it hovers for a while and then shatters.
